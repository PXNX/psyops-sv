## How do I start?

Follow the [introduction](/docs/intro). After registering, choose a region and
complete onboarding.

## How do I make money?

Work in a [factory](/docs/economy), sell goods on the market or found a company.

## How do I get involved in politics?

Join a [party](/docs/politics), run in elections and submit your own proposals.

## How do I command ships or aircraft?

Open the [navy console](/training?view=navy). The [naval](/docs/navy), [air](/docs/air) and
[map](/docs/map-modes) tutorials explain the controls step by step. You can
relaunch the interactive tours from your profile or the [docs index](/docs).

## I found a bug – what now?

Use the in-app report function and include as many details as possible.
