# An inheritance

<DocPhoto src="/images/docs/psyops.webp" alt="Psyops key art" label="An inherited fortune — and the world that wants a piece of it." />

Your uncle is dead. The lawyers have read the will, and the account is yours: a great deal of money, a handful of contracts nobody else has seen, and a network of people who used to take his calls. They are waiting to see whether you have his instinct.

You are not a bureaucrat who happens to be good at spreadsheets. You are an oligarch. In this world, force is not a single button — it is the art of applying pressure through four levers, usually in the right order:

- **Politics** — hold office, place allies in parties and parliaments, and decide who is allowed to act.
- **Force** — keep battalions, fleets and commanders ready. Military power is what makes a refusal expensive.
- **Money** — companies, factories, markets and the family fortune fund the other three.
- **Influence** — newspapers, intelligence and reputation make people choose your outcome before you have to pay for it.

<DocArt kind="levers" label="The four levers of power" caption="Every action pulls one of these levers. The art is in pulling several at once." />

Most victories begin with money, are made possible by influence, are legitimised by politics, and are only settled by force if everything else fails. The better you get at combining them, the less often you have to spend the last one.

## Your first moves

1. **Choose where you live:** your residence decides which state and region you can act in first.
2. **Look at the dock:** the console is your command centre — politics, economy, military and media all have a place there.
3. **Put your money to work:** the inheritance will not last forever. Buy or build something that produces.
4. **Get a seat at a table:** join a party, take a job in government, or start a paper. Each opens a different lever.
5. **Pick a fight carefully:** every tool in the game can be used to apply force. The trick is knowing which one is cheap.
